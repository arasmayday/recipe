In a large pot, cover the beef shanks with water and bring to a boil for about 5 minutes. Drain the water and wash the pot. Set the beef aside.
Add oliver oil, garlic, onions, ginger, szechuan chili pepper, star anise to the pot. Saute them for a few mintues until fragrant.
Add the beef back into the pot and cook each side for a couple of minutes each. 
Add cooking alcohol to the beef and cook it until most alcohol has evaporated.
Add sea kelp, bonito powder, chili bean sauce, soy sauce, sugar cubes, and water to the pot. The water should cover the beef. 
Bring the mixture to a boil, reduce heat and simmer for two hours. Turn the heat off and let it cool down.
Leave the beef soup in the fridge overnight.
Remove the beef from the soup. Cut the beef into smaller chunks. Pour the soup through a sieve into another pot. 
Add the beef back into the soup. Add tomoate, carrots and daikon to the pot. Boil the soup for another hour.
Cook noodles and leafy vegetables in a separate pot.
To serve, ladle the beef soup into bowls. Garnish with chopped green onions, cilantro, and pickled vegetables.
